# Homepage channel artwork

Retrieved 2026-10-31. WhatsApp and Viber SVGs are unmodified official brand assets,
stored locally to avoid third-party image requests.

- `whatsapp.svg`: Meta WhatsApp Brand Resource Center, digital green glyph (2026).
  Source: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/
  Archive member: `01_Glyph/01_Digital RGB/03_SVG/Digital_Glyph_Green_RGB_2026.svg`.
- `viber.svg`: Rakuten Viber Brand Center, flat purple icon (2026).
  Source: https://www.viber.com/en/brand-center/
  Archive: https://www.viber.com/app/uploads/Rakuten-Viber-Logos-2026.zip
  Archive member: `Rakuten Viber Icon/SVG/Viber Icon Flat Purple.svg`.
- `sms.svg` and `rcs.svg`: original project-owned symbols for the messaging channels;
  these are not company logos or Google Messages / RCS for Business branding.

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. Preserve their colors
and proportions when displaying them.

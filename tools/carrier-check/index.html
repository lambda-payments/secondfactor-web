Frequently Asked Questions

What is a phone carrier check?+

A carrier check identifies the phone company associated with a number, such as AT&T, Vodafone, or TELUS.

Can you identify a carrier from just a phone number?+

Yes. A lookup service can return carrier information from a phone number with its country code. The result depends on the service’s data and coverage.

Can the carrier change while the number stays the same?+

Yes. Number portability lets people keep their number when they change carriers. A number’s prefix alone may not identify its current carrier; the lookup needs current portability data.

Does a carrier check confirm that a number is valid or active?+

No. Carrier information does not confirm that a number is assigned, active, or reachable. Format validation and line status checks answer different questions.

Can a lookup tell mobile, landline, and VoIP numbers apart?+

Yes, if it includes line type information. A carrier name alone does not tell you the line type. Use the phone type checker to identify mobile, landline, or VoIP numbers.

Does a carrier check identify the owner of a number?+

No. A carrier check identifies the phone company, not the person using the number. An OTP check can confirm that a user can receive a code at that number.

Why might carrier information be unavailable?+

A lookup service may not have carrier data for a particular country or number type. Missing carrier information does not mean the number is invalid.

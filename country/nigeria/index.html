Which OTP Channels Can I Use in Nigeria?+

SecondFactor supports SMS, WhatsApp, Viber and RCS through one API. Availability in Nigeria depends on your account, sender approvals and recipient support.

How Do I Set Up OTP Delivery in Nigeria?+

Connect your chosen channels and complete their sender and template approvals. Our team can confirm the setup needed for your traffic to Nigeria.

What Happens if Delivery Fails?+

After eight seconds without confirmation, SecondFactor tries the next eligible channel enabled on your account. Review each attempt’s status and cost in your OTP logs.

Can Businesses Outside Nigeria Use SecondFactor?+

Yes. Contact our team to confirm the right channel setup and pricing for your business.

How Do I Format Phone Numbers in Nigeria?+

Use E.164 format: +234 followed by the national mobile number, with no spaces or punctuation. Omit the domestic leading zero. Example format: +2348021234567.

What Does an OTP to Nigeria Cost?+

Yes. Create an account and use your free credits in the PIE Playground to send a real OTP, inspect its route and verify the code.

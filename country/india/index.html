What Makes OTP Delivery in India Different

India has strict rules for business SMS and a large share of users on messaging apps. Both affect whether a code arrives and what it costs.

Domestic SMS Requires DLT Registration

Under TRAI's commercial communication rules, the business, its sender header and every SMS template must be registered on a telecom operator's DLT platform before domestic messages are delivered.

Messages Must Match a Template

Operators check each SMS against your registered template. A message that doesn't match, including its variable positions, can be rejected rather than delivered.

Six-Character Sender Headers

On domestic routes, users see your registered six-character header with an operator prefix and category suffix, such as VM-BRANDX-T, instead of a phone number.

Links and Callbacks Need Whitelisting

URLs, APK links and callback numbers inside an SMS must be whitelisted on DLT, and link shorteners are not allowed. Keeping OTP messages free of links avoids an extra approval step.

WhatsApp Is Widely Used

India is WhatsApp's largest market, with more than 500 million users, so many people can receive a code through an approved WhatsApp authentication template as well as by SMS.

International Traffic Works Differently

International SMS routes skip DLT but show a random numeric sender and cost more. Meta also applies a higher authentication-international rate to WhatsApp OTPs from businesses based outside India.

India OTP Compliance Checklist

Most delivery failures to Indian numbers come from registration gaps rather than the network. Check these before you go live.

This checklist summarises common requirements and is not legal advice. Rules are set by TRAI and applied by the operators; confirm the current requirements on your DLT portal.

Before go-live
· Principal entity registered on an operator DLT platform · Sender header registered for your brand · OTP content template approved, with the code as a variable · Template text matches what your application sends, character for character · OTP template includes your brand name · Any URL, APK link or callback number in the message whitelisted, with no link shorteners · Delivery chain declared between your entity and the telemarketers that carry your SMS · Resend attempts capped, since operators may block repeated messages to the same number · WhatsApp authentication template approved, if you use WhatsApp · Numbers stored and sent in +91 E.164 format

Frequently Asked Questions

Write to support@secondfactor.ai for anything not covered here.

Do I Need DLT Registration to Send OTP SMS in India?+

For domestic routes, yes. Branded SMS to Indian numbers requires your entity, sender header and message template to be registered on a telecom operator's DLT platform, and unregistered or mismatched messages can be blocked. International routes don't use DLT, but the sender appears as a random number and rates are higher.

Can OTPs Reach Numbers on India's Do Not Disturb Registry?+

Do Not Disturb preferences apply to promotional messages. OTPs registered under the appropriate service or transactional template category are delivered to numbers on the registry.

Do I Need DLT Registration for WhatsApp OTPs?+

No. WhatsApp OTPs use a WhatsApp Business Account and an approved authentication template instead of DLT. If you also enable SMS as a fallback, the SMS route still needs DLT registration.

Can My Business Send OTPs to India From Another Country?+

Yes. You can verify users with Indian numbers wherever your business is based. Traffic from businesses outside India can be priced differently, so talk to our team about your setup and expected volume.

What Happens if an SMS to an Indian Number Is Blocked or Delayed?+

If delivery isn't confirmed within eight seconds, SecondFactor attempts the next eligible channel enabled for your account. Your OTP logs show the channel, status and cost of each attempt.

How Should I Format Indian Phone Numbers?+

Use E.164 format: +91 followed by the 10-digit mobile number, with no spaces, leading zero or other prefix. For example, +919876543210.

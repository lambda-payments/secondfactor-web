Which OTP Channels Can I Use in the Philippines?+

SecondFactor supports SMS, WhatsApp, Viber and RCS through one API. Availability in the Philippines depends on your account, sender approvals and recipient support.

How Do I Set Up OTP Delivery in the Philippines?+

Connect your chosen channels and complete their sender and template approvals. Our team can confirm the setup needed for your traffic to the Philippines.

What Happens if Delivery Fails?+

After eight seconds without confirmation, SecondFactor tries the next eligible channel enabled on your account. Review each attempt’s status and cost in your OTP logs.

Can Businesses Outside the Philippines Use SecondFactor?+

Yes. Contact our team to confirm the right channel setup and pricing for your business.

How Do I Format Phone Numbers in the Philippines?+

Use E.164 format: +63 followed by the national mobile number, with no spaces or punctuation. Omit the domestic leading zero. Example format: +639051234567.

What Does an OTP to the Philippines Cost?+

The indicative rate shown above covers delivery and verification per delivered OTP. Contact sales for pricing based on your volume and channels.

Can I Test Before Integrating?+

Yes. Create an account and use your free credits in the PIE Playground to send a real OTP, inspect its route and verify the code.

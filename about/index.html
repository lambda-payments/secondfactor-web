SecondFactor is a product of Digital 360 Pte. Ltd. We provide phone verification for apps and websites through SMS, WhatsApp, Viber, and RCS.

Businesses need to deliver codes across countries where channel availability and messaging costs differ. Managing each channel separately adds integration work and makes failed deliveries harder to handle.

Our API brings those channels together. It selects an eligible route, handles fallback, and records delivery status and cost. Developers can manage verification without building a separate integration for every channel.

OUR FOCUS

Reliable delivery.
Clear costs. Less integration work.

We focus on the parts of verification that teams need to manage every day: getting codes delivered, understanding what they cost, and finding out what happened when something fails.

02 / OUR VALUES

How we make decisions

These values guide the product we build and the way we support our customers.

01

Be clear.

Explain pricing, delivery behavior, and limitations plainly. Customers should be able to understand what happened to a request and what they paid for it.

02

Build for failure.

Delivery can fail. Design for retries, fallback, and useful logs from the start. Make problems visible so they can be diagnosed and resolved.

03

Respect developers’ time.

Keep the API straightforward and the documentation practical. Reduce the work needed to integrate, test, and maintain phone verification.

04

Take responsibility.

When a customer needs help, understand the issue, explain the next steps, and follow through. Use what we learn to improve the product and its documentation.

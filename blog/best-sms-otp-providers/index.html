Key takeaways

  • SMS reaches users without an app or data connection, which is why it remains the default OTP channel.
  • Pricing varies widely by destination country, and message length and encoding affect how many segments you pay for.
  • Sender registration rules differ by market and can delay go-live.

SMS works on every handset and needs no app, which makes it the baseline for one-time passcodes. It is also the channel where cost and deliverability vary most from country to country.

How we evaluated

We compared each provider against the criteria below, using publicly available documentation and pricing.

  • Country coverage. Whether the provider delivers reliably to the specific countries and carriers you serve.
  • Pricing. Per-message rates in your main destinations, and how message segments are billed.
  • Sender registration. Support for branded sender IDs and the registration some markets require.
  • Deliverability and fallback. How delivery is confirmed and what happens when it is not.
  • Additional channels. Whether the same integration can add WhatsApp, Viber or RCS later.

How to choose

The best option depends on your situation more than on any ranking. Start from the constraint that matters most to you.

If you send to a handful of countries

Compare rates and sender requirements for exactly those destinations. A provider that is strong in your markets beats one with the longest coverage list.

If you send globally

Prioritise breadth of coverage, clear sender registration support and per-request delivery visibility.

If SMS costs are climbing

Consider a provider that can route eligible requests through WhatsApp, Viber or RCS and keep SMS as the fallback.

Frequently asked questions

Do users need an app or internet connection to receive an SMS OTP?

No. A standard SMS needs only a mobile number with SMS service, although completing verification in your app may require a connection.

Can I use my business name as the SMS sender?

It depends on the destination. Some markets require registration and approval before a business name can appear as the sender.

How much does an SMS OTP cost?

Pricing depends on the destination country and your pricing arrangement. Message length and encoding can also affect how many segments are billed.

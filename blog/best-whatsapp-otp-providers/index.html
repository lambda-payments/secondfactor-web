Key takeaways

  • You need a WhatsApp Business Account and approved authentication templates before sending WhatsApp OTPs with any provider.
  • Not every user has WhatsApp, so fallback to another channel is essential.
  • WhatsApp is not always cheaper than SMS. The lower-cost option depends on the destination.

In many markets WhatsApp is where users already read their messages, which makes it a natural channel for verification codes. It also comes with rules: authentication templates use prescribed wording, and you need a connected WhatsApp Business Account before anything is sent.

This guide compares providers that offer WhatsApp OTP delivery, focusing on setup effort, template handling, pricing and fallback. Provider details were last checked on the date above.

How to choose

The best option depends on your situation more than on any ranking. Start from the constraint that matters most to you.

If you already have a WhatsApp Business Account

Look for a provider that lets you connect your existing account rather than creating a new one, so your sender identity carries over.

If you need SMS fallback

Choose a provider where fallback is automatic and uses the same API, so an undelivered WhatsApp code does not become a failed login.

If you are starting from scratch

Factor in onboarding time. Account connection and template approval happen before any code is sent, whichever provider you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a WhatsApp Business Account to send OTPs?

Yes. You need to connect a WhatsApp Business Account and complete the applicable sender setup and authentication template approvals before sending WhatsApp OTPs.

Can I customise the WhatsApp OTP message?

Only within limits. WhatsApp authentication templates use prescribed wording. Supported options include a security disclaimer and an expiry warning.

What happens if the user does not have WhatsApp?

The message cannot be delivered on that channel. Providers with multi-channel fallback can attempt another eligible channel, such as SMS, instead.

Is WhatsApp always cheaper than SMS for OTPs?

No. The lower-cost option depends on the destination country and the applicable rates.

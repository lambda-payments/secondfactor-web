What Is OTP Template Management?

An OTP template is the message that carries a verification code. Each channel has its own rules for it: an SMS template is short text with a code placeholder, while a WhatsApp authentication template follows wording set by WhatsApp.

Template management is how you create those messages, submit them for review and keep track of which ones are approved. SecondFactor keeps the templates for all your connected channels in one dashboard.

Once a template is approved, your application refers to it by ID, or sends without one and uses your default.

How Template Setup Works

Four steps take a template from draft to live delivery.

Create the Template

Write the message for a connected channel with your app name and the OTP placeholder, following that channel’s format.

Submit It for Review

Templates are reviewed before live traffic. Requirements and timing depend on the channel and destination.

Track Its Status

Follow the template’s approval status in your dashboard until it is ready to use.

Send With It

Send with your default template, or pass a template ID to choose one for that request.

Choose a Template in the Send Request

TemplateSid is optional. Leave it out and SecondFactor uses your default template. Include it to use one of the templates listed on your dashboard’s Templates page.

A send with no TemplateSid and no default template is refused with no_default_template. An ID that isn’t one of your templates is refused with unknown_template. Refused sends are not charged.

Moving from Twilio Verify? A Twilio template SID (HJ…) is ignored, so those requests send with your default template.

Frequently Asked Questions

Write to support@secondfactor.ai for anything not covered here.

Do I Need a Template Before Sending an OTP?+

Yes. A send needs either a TemplateSid or a default template on your account. Without one, the request is refused and not charged.

Can I Write My Own OTP Message?+

It depends on the channel. SMS and Viber templates take your own wording with your app name and an OTP placeholder, subject to review. WhatsApp authentication templates use prescribed wording with configurable options.

How Long Does Template Approval Take?+

Approval timing depends on the channel and its review process. Check the template’s status in your dashboard before using it for live delivery.

Can I Use One Template Across Every Channel?+

You manage templates together, but the content has to follow each channel’s rules, so channels can need separate templates.

Can I Use Different Templates for Signup and Login?+

Yes. Pass a different TemplateSid with each send to choose the template for that verification.

What Happens to My Twilio Template SIDs?+

A Twilio template SID in the request is ignored and the send uses your default template.

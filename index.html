FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something not covered? Write to support@secondfactor.ai.

How does SecondFactor choose a delivery channel?+

The Price Intelligence Engine checks the destination and eligible channels, then attempts delivery through the lowest-cost option. If delivery isn't confirmed within eight seconds, it tries the next eligible channel.

Do I need to connect every channel?+

You can start with the channels you need and add others later. SecondFactor routes requests through channels enabled for your account and available to the recipient.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business Account to send WhatsApp OTPs?+

Yes. You need to connect a WhatsApp Business Account and complete the applicable setup and authentication template approvals before sending WhatsApp OTPs.

Do I need to generate and store OTPs in my application?+

No. SecondFactor handles code generation and verification. Use /otp/send to request delivery and /otp/verify to check the code your user entered.

Can I test SecondFactor before going live?+

New accounts receive free credits to test real OTP delivery in the PIE Playground, inspect the selected route and verify the code received.

Can I see whether an OTP was delivered?+

Your dashboard includes OTP logs showing the destination, delivery channel, status and cost of each request.

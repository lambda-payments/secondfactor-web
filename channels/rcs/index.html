Frequently Asked Questions

Write to support@secondfactor.ai for anything not covered here.

Can Every Phone Receive RCS OTPs?+

RCS business message availability depends on the recipient's device, messaging setup, carrier, and market. SecondFactor checks recipient eligibility before attempting RCS delivery.

Do Recipients Need WhatsApp or Viber?+

Recipients receive RCS messages through a compatible phone messaging app. They do not need WhatsApp or Viber, but RCS must be supported and available on their device.

Do I Need an Approved RCS Business Sender?+

Yes, you need to register an RCS business sender, also called an agent, and complete the applicable approval process before sending live messages.

How Long Does RCS Setup Take?+

Setup includes a sender application and review, so it is not immediate. Our team can explain the requirements and expected timing for your application.

What Happens if a Recipient Does Not Support RCS?+

RCS is excluded from the eligible delivery options, allowing SecondFactor to select another available channel enabled for your account.

What Happens if RCS Delivery Isn't Confirmed?+

SecondFactor attempts the next eligible channel after eight seconds without delivery confirmation. Available fallback options depend on your connected channels and the recipient's capabilities.

Is RCS Always Cheaper Than SMS?+

The lower-cost option depends on destination pricing and applicable rates. SecondFactor evaluates eligible routes for each request rather than always choosing RCS.

Do I Need a Separate API Integration for RCS?+

Your application uses the same SecondFactor send-and-verify API across supported channels. RCS requires its own sender and template setup, but no separate delivery integration in your application.
